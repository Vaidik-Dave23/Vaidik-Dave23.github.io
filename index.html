02RAG benchmarking

DocMind

Multi-document RAG and the harness that measures it, on a dense 502-page legal judgment.

  • 4 chunking strategies benchmarked. Naive fixed-size beat semantic and hierarchical on context precision.
  • RAGAS-style evaluator: batched judge prompts, local MiniLM similarity, SHA-256 score cache.
  • Embeddings cached at upload, which fixed a major latency issue in compare mode.
