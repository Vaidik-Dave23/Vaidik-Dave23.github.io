  • Built a cascading LLM router: requests walk up to 19 shuffled Gemini keys across two Gemini models before falling back to GPT-4o-mini. Keys that hit their daily quota are blacklisted until UTC midnight; per-minute 429s skip straight to the next key.
  • Wrote an LLM-as-judge evaluator that scores RAG answers for faithfulness and relevance: 81% faithfulness and 99% relevance on a 19-question benchmark, up from the mid-60s once the prompt forbade outside knowledge, and 76% across 46 real production queries.
  • Built PDF RAG chat on a custom markdown-aware chunker, Gemini embeddings and per-user cosine retrieval; notes, quizzes and roadmaps generate from topics, PDFs or YouTube videos.
  • Instrumented every request with structured JSON logs, request IDs, Prometheus metrics for LLM latency and tokens per provider/model, and OpenTelemetry traces in Grafana and Jaeger.
  • Shipped on Azure App Service (CI/CD via GitHub Actions) and Vercel, with MongoDB Atlas, Google OAuth + JWT and per-user rate limiting.

DocMind

Multi-document RAG engine with a retrieval benchmark over a 502-page legal corpus.

Source code →
FIG. 2 · RETRIEVAL PIPELINE502 PAGES
  • Built multi-document RAG over a 502-page legal corpus with FAISS and all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings.
  • Rebuilt a RAGAS-style faithfulness evaluator with embedding-based caching, cutting LLM calls per evaluated query from 8 to 2.
  • Benchmarked fixed-size against semantic chunking on Recall@k and MRR, and found the winner flips with how gold labels are defined. Traced the semantic chunker's misses to 19 of 83 chunks running past MiniLM's 256-token limit, where evidence was silently truncated.
  • Diagnosed and fixed a silent retrieval failure caused by a metadata schema mismatch between the chunk store and the query layer.
